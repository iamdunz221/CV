Core Competencies

Structured capabilities aligned directly with standard IT Business Analyst roles, bridging requirements engineering, system modeling, and technical validation.

Business Analysis

  • Requirements Elicitation: Stakeholder interviews, surveys, and requirement clarification.
  • Documentation: BRD, SRS , and Functional/Non-Functional Specs (FR/NFR).
  • Agile Stories: User Stories with concrete Acceptance Criteria (Given-When-Then).
