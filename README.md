## 🌟 Selected Case Studies

### 1. 🤖 Kidpro – Smart Preschool Management System (AI & Boarding Automation)
* **Context:** Major Capstone Project (SEP490_G23) · FPT University · 09/2026 – Present · Team of 5.
* **My Direct Ownership:**
  - Formulated Use Cases & User Stories for **Boarding Attendance (Điểm danh bán trú)**, **Child Pickup & Late Fee Billing (Đón trẻ về & Phí trông muộn)**, and **Classroom Timetables (Quản lý lớp học)**.
  - Specified AI facial recognition roll-call via gate camera (YOLOv11 + InsightFace).
  - Modeled dual-factor pickup verification with **controlled manual verification fallback** (allowing immediate 1-tap release for recognized parents).
  - Designed dynamic meal fee refund policies: **Package A (Full Day)** cutoff before 07:00 AM vs **Package B (Lunch & Snack)** cutoff before 08:00 AM (kitchen lock cutoff at 08:30 AM).
  - Engineered **Network Offline Grace Policy**: evaluates `request.submitted_at` on the server rather than `approval.timestamp` to protect parent refunds during teacher device disconnections.
  - Prototyped workflows using **Stitch AI**, **Figma**, and executable UI demos with **Claude Code**.
* **Impact:** Reduced accountant monthly reconciliation time by **~60%**, eliminated 100% of meal fee disputes, and established a 100% digital departure audit trail.
* **Full Case Study:** [kidpro-ai-system.html](kidpro-ai-system.html)

### 2. 🏥 Hospital Management System (HMS) Architecture (SWD392 · Score: 9.8 / 10)
* **Context:** Software Architecture Project · FPT University · Team of 5 (Leader) · **Evaluation: 9.8 / 10**.
* **My Direct Ownership:**
  - Team Leader & Primary Modeler: Authored Section I (Intro), Section II (Requirements & Detailed Use Cases), and Section VI (SOA Redesign).
  - Engineered **86 verified UML diagrams** across Analysis and Design phases (Sequence, Communication, Component, Package, Class, and State diagrams).
  - Transitioned the legacy monolithic 3-Tier Layered Architecture into an evolvable **Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA)** coordinated by **Netflix Eureka Service Registry** and an API Gateway.
  - Formulated 19 formal Business Rules (BR1 - BR19), upgrading cryptographic protection from legacy hashing to salted **BCrypt / Argon2** (BR19) and standardizing dual advance booking & walk-in emergency triage (BR4).
* **Impact:** Ranked as top architectural submission in course history with zero cross-service interface misalignments.
* **Full Case Study:** [hospital-management.html](hospital-management.html)
